CASE STUDY 01 · MBA HACKATHON

Ecommerce Shopping App

Building a local commerce experience around ONDC, local vendors and customer trust.

The Problem

Local stores are losing market share to centralized quick-commerce dark stores. We needed to conceptualize a solution that leverages the ONDC network to digitally empower neighborhood shops without destroying community economics.

Design Solution

Mapped the emotional and logistical needs of the "conscious consumer". Architected a 2-way marketplace (supply and demand side) focused on seamless onboarding and localized discovery.

Challenges Faced

Bridging the technological gap for local vendors who are not tech-savvy, and building trust for consumers accustomed to polished big-box platforms.

Resolution

Designed a frictionless vendor interface focused on simple inventory toggles, and created a consumer-facing loyalty framework verifying local authenticity.

Measuring Success

Evaluated through vendor onboarding completion rates, 90-day pilot runaway metrics, repeat customer engagement, and projected breakeven milestones.

Business Insight: Customers want authenticity and support for local businesses, but they will not compromise on digital convenience and discoverability. The platform must bridge this exact gap.

CASE STUDY 02

Social & Multiplayer Experience

Turning player relationships into clear, actionable journeys in Ludo game.

The Problem

Players needed a simple way to find people, send requests, respond to invitations and move from social actions into gameplay without friction or confusion.

Design Solution

Used familiar notification patterns and clear, actionable buttons. Mapped the entire journey to reduce steps before entering gameplay and support different player states.

Challenges Faced

Handling multiple attack scenarios (random player, revenge, friend selection) which had very different entry points but needed to feel cohesive.

Resolution

Created a unified attack UI component that adapts dynamically based on the entry point while maintaining the same core interaction flow.

Measuring Success

Tracked feature adoption rates, daily active multiplayer matches, and the drop-off rate in the challenge-to-play funnel.

CASE STUDY 03

Plant Discovery & Collection

A calmer product experience built around discovery, browsing and detail.

The Problem

Plant discovery apps often overwhelm users with dense, cluttered botanical data, making the experience stressful rather than relaxing.

Design Solution

Adopted a soft visual hierarchy, large plant imagery, and simple bottom navigation to keep the focus on discovery and create a calming environment.

Challenges Faced

Balancing beautiful, high-quality imagery with useful, detailed care information (water, sunlight, soil) without cluttering the screen.

Resolution

Implemented a progressive disclosure system where primary care icons are visible at a glance, and deep-dive botanical data is accessible only when expanded.

Measuring Success

Measured by an increase in average session length, user retention rate (Day-7), and the average number of plants added to personal collections.

CASE STUDY 04

Furniture Commerce Flow

A complete path from product discovery to completed order.

The Problem

High consideration purchases like furniture suffer from high cart abandonment due to friction, lack of clarity, and decision fatigue during customization.

Design Solution

Designed an end-to-end commerce flow focused on transparency. Broke down complex product details and customization into a digestible step-by-step process.

Challenges Faced

Making customization clear without causing decision fatigue, and maintaining trust regarding delivery logistics and hidden costs.

Resolution

Grouped customization options logically, provided real-time visual feedback, and surfaced delivery timelines and full pricing upfront before the cart step.

Measuring Success

Tracked cart abandonment reduction, successful end-to-end checkout completion rates, and an increase in overall conversion for customized items.
